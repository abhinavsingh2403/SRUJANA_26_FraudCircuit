REVIEW OUTCOMES

What analysts recorded

Outcomes are human assessments on selected cases. They do not establish detection accuracy, recall or population false-positive rate.

MEASUREMENT BOUNDARY

What is and is not measured

No labelled evaluation runs on this page. Operational counts are computed from the current database. Synthetic fixtures test behavior; they do not validate real-world fraud detection.

Active priority policy & settings

Priority determines review order, not a probability or verdict. Context may lower priority under the explicit routine-pattern policy; it never removes the underlying risk evidence.
