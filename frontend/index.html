Tamper-evident review log

Review decisions are stored with a per-case SHA-256 hash chain so accidental or partial changes can be detected. Without an external anchor a database owner can rewrite the whole chain or delete its tail.

Priority classification policy

Priorities represent analyst review order, not fraud probabilities or verdicts.

PriorityMeaningRequired signals
HIGH Review first Requires structural motif (Cycle, Chain, Value Burst) AND corroborating rule signals linked across transactions.
MEDIUM Investigation required At least one primary risk rule triggered (e.g. Rapid Pass-Through, Fan-in Dispersal, Amount Deviation).
LOW Routine / Context Signals flagged by baseline rules but accompanied by benign settlement patterns or low deviation.
INSUFFICIENT HISTORY Coverage limitation Target account baseline history is shorter than 30 days. This is a coverage boundary, not a fraud signal.
